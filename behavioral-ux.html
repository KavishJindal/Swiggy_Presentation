Behavioral UX Architecture

The Checkout Funnel, Nudge Theory, and Regulatory Scrutiny

The Storefront as a Choice Engine

Modern platforms do not passively display menus. They employ structured UI nudges—leveraging cognitive biases from Kahneman & Tversky's Prospect Theory—to elevate the Average Order Value (AOV) and radically shift margin profiles.

Core UX Nudges

1. Drip Pricing & JND

Revealing the mandatory flat fee (e.g., ₹2) late in checkout leverages the Just Noticeable Difference. Sunk-cost prevents cart abandonment.

2. Basket-Building Thresholds

"Add ₹35 more to unlock Free Delivery." Users perceive delivery fees as a loss, prompting the addition of high-margin impulse items.

3. Choice Friction

Multi-step warnings for 100% cancellation fees protect margins. Chatbot decision trees obscure expensive live human support.

4. Sunk-Cost Subscription

Paying upfront for Swiggy One psychologically zeroes out the marginal delivery cost, drastically curtailing competitor "multi-homing".
