Financials & Revenue

Monetization Streams, Cost Structures, and Q1 FY2027 Performance

The 7 Core Revenue Streams

To decouple from sole reliance on merchant commissions, Swiggy has aggressively diversified its income matrix.

1. Commissions

15-25% of order value. Exclusive partners receive a ~2% visibility reduction.

2. Delivery Charges

Distance & weather-based fees, heavily nudging users towards larger basket sizes.

3. Advertising

High-margin priority search listings and banner promotions sold to restaurants.

4. Swiggy One

Subscription cash flow that drastically cuts user multi-homing on rival apps.

5. Swiggy Access

Cloud kitchen infrastructure scaling partner reach into unserved neighborhoods.

6. Own Brands

Private label restaurants providing ultimate quality, margin, and data control.

7. Affiliate Income

Financial tie-ups (Swiggy HDFC Bank Card) capturing off-platform spend data.

Primary Cost Centers

Logistics & Payouts

Delivery partner incentives, fuel, and weather-surge subsidies (highly variable).

Physical Infrastructure

Dark store leases for Instamart operations and cloud kitchen spaces.

Customer Acquisition

Marketing budgets, aggressive discounting, and retention cash-burn.

Tech & Operations

Server costs, app maintenance, corporate payroll, and automated refunds.
