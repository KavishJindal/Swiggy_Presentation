Executive Summary

Project Origins, Core Abstract, and Evolution Milestones

Project Abstract

This analytical project examines how Swiggy, one of India's two dominant food delivery platforms, creates, delivers, and captures value. Taking the foundational 2022 case study by Prashar, Bawa, and Kaur as a base, we test its historical description against the aggressive modern developments up to Swiggy's Q1 FY2027 results (April-June 2026).

The analysis deconstructs Swiggy's evolution from a simple two-sided aggregator earning base commissions, into a complex ecosystem where quick commerce (Instamart) challenges food delivery in gross order volume. Crucially, the project investigates how Swiggy is shifting towards unit profitability via zero-marginal-cost digital levers: Behavioral UX choice architecture and Retail Media Networks (RMN).

Evolution Milestones

2014: The Launch

Founded in Bangalore. Began with just 25 partner restaurants and 6 delivery executives.

2019-2020: Diversification

Launch of Swiggy Stores, Swiggy Go (Genie), and the pioneering of 10-30 min quick commerce via Instamart.

2023: Unit Economics

Introduction of the mandatory flat ₹2 platform fee to drive localized unit profitability.

Nov 2024: Public Markets

Listed on NSE/BSE. Massive IPO of ₹11,327 Cr, valuing the company at ₹87,300 Cr.

Q1 2026: Scale

1,171 active dark stores. Food delivery officially hits adjusted EBITDA profitability.
