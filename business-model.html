The Business Model

Platform Economics, Network Effects, and Order Journeys

Two-Sided Platform Dynamics

Swiggy operates as a central digital infrastructure connecting three main pillars. Its success relies entirely on network density: a localized market is only viable if there are enough restaurants to attract users, and enough delivery partners to fulfill those orders rapidly.

Business Model Canvas

Value Proposition Convenience and speed for users; logistics and visibility for merchants; flexible earning for gig workers.
Key Resources The technology stack, the 1,171+ dark store network (Instamart), brand equity, and vast delivery fleets.
Customer Relationships Driven by self-service UI, automated grievance handling, and behavioral loyalty lock-in (Swiggy One).
Key Partnerships Exclusive restaurant partners, enterprise FMCG brands, and financial institutions (HDFC, Citibank).
